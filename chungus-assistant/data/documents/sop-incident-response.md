# SOP-022: Security Incident Response

**Owner:** IT Security · **Classification:** Internal · **Last reviewed:** 2026-02-28

## Purpose
Steps every employee follows when they suspect a security incident (lost device, phishing, data exposure).

## Procedure
1. **Stop and preserve.** Do not delete anything or attempt your own fix.
2. **Report within 1 hour** to the Security on-call at security-oncall@chungus.example or ext. x4009.
3. Provide: what happened, when, which systems or data, and any error messages.
4. The on-call engineer opens a ticket and assigns a severity (SEV-1 highest to SEV-4 lowest).
5. For SEV-1/SEV-2, the Incident Commander convenes a bridge call within 30 minutes.
6. A written post-incident review is completed within 5 business days of resolution.

## Reminders
- Never share credentials or passwords over email or chat, even during an incident.
- Never move suspected-compromised data to a personal device or account.

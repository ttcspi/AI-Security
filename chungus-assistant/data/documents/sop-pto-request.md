# SOP-008: Paid Time Off (PTO) Requests

**Owner:** People Ops · **Classification:** Internal · **Last reviewed:** 2026-01-30

## Purpose
How Chungus employees request and track paid time off.

## Procedure
1. Submit PTO in the People Ops portal at least 10 business days before the start date.
2. Requests over 5 consecutive days need manager approval; shorter requests are auto-approved if coverage exists.
3. PTO accrues at 1.5 days per month worked, capped at 30 days carried over year to year.
4. Sick leave is separate and does not require advance notice.

## Notes
- Public holidays follow the schedule posted each January by People Ops.
- Unused PTO above the cap is paid out at year end at your base rate.

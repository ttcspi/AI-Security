# Chungus Employee Handbook — Code of Conduct (excerpt)

**Owner:** People Ops · **Classification:** Internal · **Last reviewed:** 2026-01-05

## Our standards
Chungus employees treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. We do not tolerate
harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind.

## Confidentiality
Employees handle company and colleague information on a need-to-know basis. Personal data about
other employees (compensation, home contact details, government identifiers) is confidential and
must not be shared without an explicit business need and People Ops approval.

## Acceptable use
Company systems are for company business. Do not use them to access, store, or transmit offensive
material, or to circumvent security controls.

## Raising concerns
Report conduct concerns to People Ops (people-help@chungus.example) or the anonymous ethics line
listed on the intranet. Good-faith reports are protected from retaliation.

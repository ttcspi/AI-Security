# SOP-014: Expense Reimbursement

**Owner:** Finance · **Classification:** Internal · **Last reviewed:** 2026-01-12

## Purpose
Defines how Chungus employees submit and receive reimbursement for approved business expenses.

## Procedure
1. Submit expenses in the Chungus Expense Portal within 30 days of the spend date.
2. Attach an itemized receipt for every line over $25.
3. Your direct manager approves or rejects within 5 business days.
4. Finance issues payment to your payroll account on the next fortnightly cycle.

## Limits
- Meals: up to $60/day domestic, $90/day international.
- Ground transport: economy/standard fares only.
- Anything above $2,000 requires prior written approval from a department head.
